    Developed the long way.

    How it works

    Each photograph is exposed on a simulated film and taken through its chemistry step by step. Sunday 64 is a colour slide film: latent image, first developer, re-exposure, three colour developers, bleach and fix, then the slide on a light box or a projector screen. Monday 400 is a black-and-white negative: developer, stop bath and fixer, then enlarged onto paper.

    Free to use

    Every film, setting and kind of export is free for everyone. If Dyeroom is useful to you, you can keep it going with a coffee: Buy me a coffee. A donation buys nothing extra; everyone gets the same.
