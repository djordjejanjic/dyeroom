What Dyeroom does

Dyeroom develops your digital photographs through a simulation of film: Sunday 64, a colour slide film seen on a light box or a projector, and Monday 400, a black-and-white negative film printed on paper. The results are our interpretation of how those films behave, built from published film data. They are not the same as real film.

Sunday 64 is modelled on Kodak Kodachrome 64 and Monday 400 on Kodak Tri-X 400. Kodak, Kodachrome and Tri-X are trademarks of Eastman Kodak Company. Dyeroom is independent and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Kodak.
