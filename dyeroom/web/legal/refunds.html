Refund policy

Last updated {{updated}}

If Dyeroom isn’t right for you, you can ask for a full refund within {{refund_days}} days of a payment, for any reason. That goes for a roll, a plan’s first payment and each renewal.

How to ask

Paddle.com, our reseller and Merchant of Record, handles payments and refunds. Ask at paddle.net, where you can find your purchase with the email address you paid with, or through the link in the receipt Paddle sent you. You can also write to us at {{contact_email}} and we will arrange it. The money goes back the way you paid, usually within a few days of the refund being approved.

What happens to your film

  • When a roll is refunded, the frames from it that you haven’t used are taken off your account. Photographs you already developed and exported are yours to keep.
  • When a plan payment is refunded, the plan ends.

Cancelling a plan

You can cancel a plan at any time from Account, Plan and receipts. It runs to the end of the period you have paid for, and you are not charged again.

Photographs that didn’t develop

If a photograph can’t be developed at all, its frame comes back to your account by itself. There is nothing to ask for.

Your legal rights

This policy adds to your rights as a consumer, including any right to withdraw from a purchase where you live; it doesn’t take any away.
