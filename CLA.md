Version 1

started and maintains the project (“the maintainer”). You keep the copyright in what you
contribute. In return for accepting your contribution, the maintainer asks for a licence broad

the maintainer's promise back to you.

**You** are the person who signs this agreement, or the organisation on whose behalf you sign
it. **Contribution** means any work you submit to the Dyeroom project, in its repositories,
issues, pull requests or otherwise, that you intend to be included in it: code, data,
documentation, images or anything else. **Submit** means any form of communication to the
maintainer or the project, electronic, verbal or written.

Your Contributions are provided “as is”, without warranties or conditions of any kind. The
maintainer does not have to use or include any Contribution.

## How to sign

On your first pull request, a bot asks you to sign. Reply in the pull request with the
sentence:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

Your GitHub user name, the date and the pull request are recorded in the repository (in the
`cla-signatures` branch). The agreement then covers all your Contributions to Dyeroom.
